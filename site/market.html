Where the index stands

The ICE BofA option-adjusted spread for BBB and BB corporates, the benchmarks most new deals here are measured against. A wider index means credit is cheaper across the board.

Does pricing around big data cost more?

A data day is the day of a Fed decision, CPI report or jobs report, or the business day before it. Each senior unsecured bond priced on one is compared with senior unsecured bonds of the same rating and maturity priced on other days, using spread vs the index that day, so a market-wide move does not count as a concession.

Cheap or rich, by tenor

Each dot is one bond: its spread minus the matching index on the day it priced. Above the dashed line it paid more than comparable paper.
