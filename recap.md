The week brought $1.45 billion across 3 tranches, a sharp drop from the prior four weeks when volume ranged from $1.5 billion to $50.0 billion and tranche counts from 2 to 61. Atmos Energy Corporation led with $700 million of 10-year notes at 78 basis points, Hercules Capital priced $400 million 3-year at 195, and Trinity Capital issued $350 million 5-year at 262.5. All three priced wide versus their index benchmarks. Trinity Capital priced widest at +159.5 basis points through the index on its Baa3-rated 7.500% 2032s, followed by Hercules Capital at +91 on its Baa2-rated notes. Even Atmos Energy, rated A2/A-, came 8 basis points wide on its 10-year tranche. The pattern is consistent: specialty finance names paid a concession, and even the higher-quality utility issuer gave up a few basis points to get the deal done. Only Atmos Energy disclosed a use of proceeds, stating general corporate purposes, while Hercules Capital and Trinity Capital left proceeds unstated in the filings.
