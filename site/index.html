Does the bond market price what's in the prospectus?

A prospectus spells out where a bond ranks, what backs it and what protects its holders. If investors read it, those terms should show up in the spread. This tracker tests five of them against every registered US corporate bond deal, and updates as new deals price.

The test

Each bond is compared with peers that lack the feature: first the same issuer's other bonds sold the same day, otherwise bonds with the same rating and maturity. The dot is the median difference; the bar is the range across 1,000 resamples by issuer. A bar that crosses zero means the data cannot yet tell.
