Latest week in the data

The week brought $1.45 billion across 3 tranches, a sharp drop from the prior four weeks when volume ranged from $1.5 billion to $50.0 billion and tranche counts from 2 to 61. Atmos Energy Corporation led with $700 million of 10-year notes at 78 basis points, Hercules Capital priced $400 million 3-year at 195, and Trinity Capital issued $350 million 5-year at 262.5. All three priced wide versus their index benchmarks. Trinity Capital priced widest at 159.5 basis points wide of the index on its Baa3-rated 7.500% 2032s, followed by Hercules Capital at +91 on its Baa2-rated notes. Even Atmos Energy, rated A2/A-, came 8 basis points wide on its 10-year tranche. The pattern is consistent: specialty finance names paid a concession, and even the higher-quality utility issuer gave up a few basis points to get the deal done. Only Atmos Energy disclosed a use of proceeds, stating general corporate purposes, while Hercules Capital and Trinity Capital left proceeds unstated in the filings.

How each claim is tested

  • Spread vs index. Each bond's spread to Treasuries minus the ICE BofA option-adjusted index spread for its rating on the day it priced. Positive means it came wider, which is cheaper for the buyer.
  • Peers. A bond with the feature is compared first with the same issuer's bonds sold the same day in the same maturity bucket, on raw spread, which holds the company constant. Failing that, with bonds that lack the feature in the same rating bucket (AA, A, BBB, BB) and maturity bucket, on spread vs index.
  • Holding ranking constant. The change of control and coupon step claims compare senior unsecured bonds only, so they do not pick up subordination or collateral.
  • Range. The 5th to 95th percentile of the median across 1,000 resamples. Issuers are resampled whole, because twelve bonds from one deal are one piece of evidence, not twelve.
  • Verdict. Holding if the whole range sits on the expected side of zero, Not holding if it sits on the other side, Mixed if it crosses zero. Too early below 5 bonds or 3 issuers.
  • Trend. The estimate recomputed with only the deals priced by the end of each week, so you can see whether a reading is settling or still moving.

Limits

  • Registered deals only. Most high yield is sold under Rule 144A and never reaches EDGAR, so HY samples are small.
  • Index spreads are matched by rating category, not exact notch, and do not adjust for sector or call features.
  • Covenants are not assigned at random: issuers offer protection when buyers ask for it, so a feature's premium partly reflects the kind of company that offers it.
  • Filings do not disclose order books, price talk or the new issue concession.
  • 208 of 215 tranches have a stated ranking. 9 failed the pricing math check and are flagged, not discarded.

How it is built

Findbackfill.py scans EDGAR free writing prospectuses for bond pricing term sheets.
Extractextract.py has Claude pull each tranche's terms, using only numbers printed in the filing.
Structurestructure.py finds each deal's prospectus supplement and reads ranking, guarantees, change of control and coupon steps, checked against the notes' own titles.
Proceedsproceeds.py classifies use of proceeds with a verbatim quote.
CheckEvery tranche must satisfy benchmark yield + spread = yield. dedupe.py merges a deal filed twice.
Enrichfred.py and enrich.py pull index spreads and Treasury yields from FRED.
Testthesis.py runs the matched comparisons and resampling behind the scorecard.
Writeagent.py drafts the weekly note, limited to numbers its database queries return.
Publishreport.py builds this site.
